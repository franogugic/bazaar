# Bazaar: Product Brief

## What is Bazaar

Bazaar is an app for buying and selling many kinds of items. It is made for buyers and sellers. Listings are the center of the app. Logged-in users can buy and sell products, using an internal wallet instead of real payments.

## Users

- **Sellers:** advertise their products to potential buyers.
- **Buyers:** browse listings and buy products.

## Core features

- **Profile creation:** every user first has to create an account with their personal data.
- **Listings:** the base of the app. A seller creates a listing with a title, a price, photos and a description.
- **Checkout:** a buyer buys a listing. The item is reserved, the buyer pays, and an order is created.
- **Wallet:** every user has a balance in the internal wallet. Money moves between users inside the app only, not through real payments.

## Later

- **Search:** a search engine, to make finding listings easier.
- **Recommendations:** a system that suggests similar products.
- **Seller dashboard:** analytics for sellers.
- **Follow sellers and home feed:** users follow sellers and see their new listings in one feed.
- **Offline drafts:** a seller can edit a listing draft offline and sync it later.
- **Fraud signals:** automatic checks that find suspicious activity.
- **Data export and deletion:** a user can download all their data and ask for it to be deleted.

## Out of scope

- **Delivery:** the focus of the app is selling products, not delivering them.
- **Real payments:** the app uses an internal wallet instead.
- **Mobile app:** for now, the app is available on the web only.
